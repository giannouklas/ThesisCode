# HVAC Schedule Optimization for Office Buildings

Comparison of optimization techniques for scheduling the HVAC system of two
office rooms (Office 12 and Office 35) in central Athens. The goal is to
maintain thermal comfort while minimizing HVAC energy consumption.

## Input data

Hourly time series of:
- indoor temperature
- HVAC energy consumption
- outdoor temperature

## Project overview

The project can be broken down into three stages, each implemented in its own notebook(s):

1. **Preprocessing**
   - Fill missing values in the time series.
   - Estimate the binary (ON/OFF) HVAC operation series from the energy
     consumption series.
2. **Indoor temperature forecasting**
   - Develop and compare models (MLR, LightGBM, neural networks) that predict
     indoor temperature from its past values (lags) and exogenous variables.
3. **HVAC schedule optimization**
   - Optimize the HVAC schedule over 24-hour horizons for winter and summer.
   - Methods compared: Greedy, MILP, MIQP, Genetic Algorithm (GA), Simulated
     Annealing (SA).

## Input Data files

| File | Type | Description |
|------|------|-------------|
| `consumption12.xlsx` | Raw | Energy consumption, Office 12 |
| `consumption35.xlsx` | Raw | Energy consumption, Office 35 |

Only the raw files are original data. All other Excel files are generated by
the notebooks and can be recreated by running them in order.
